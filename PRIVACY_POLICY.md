# Droidrops Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-12

Droidrops is an on-device RSS reader. It does not send user data to the developer or operate its own backend.

What the app does
- Stores application settings locally on your device.
- Lets you configure RSS feeds and third-party services such as FreshRSS, Nextcloud News, Fever, and Google Reader-compatible APIs.
- Sends requests only to the services and URLs you configure.

Data handling
- Feed URLs, account URLs, usernames, passwords, and synchronization settings are stored locally on your device.
- When you use the app, that data is sent to the RSS service or website you chose, because that is required for the app to work.
- Droidrops does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash reporting services.
- Droidrops does not collect telemetry or sell personal data.

Third-party services
- If you use a third-party RSS service or website, that service has its own privacy policy and terms.
- The developer of Droidrops does not control how third-party services process your data.

Contact
- For questions or privacy concerns, open an issue on the project repository: https://github.com/thekester/Droidrops/issues
